## Priority Maharashtra Crop List for the Initial Pilot

### Major Kharif crops
- Paddy (Rice)
- Jowar (Sorghum)
- Bajra (Pearl millet)
- Maize (Corn)
- Tur / Arhar (Pigeon pea)
- Groundnut
- Soybean
- Cotton

### Major Rabi crops
- Wheat
- Gram (Chickpea)
- Rapeseed & Mustard
- Safflower

### High-value commercial crops
- Sugarcane
- Onion
- Chilli
- Turmeric

### Major orchard / perennial crops
- Orange (Nagpur mandarin)
- Banana
- Mango
- Sapota

### Excluded from the initial scope
The following are lower-priority for the first model and can be removed from the initial pilot list to reduce ambiguity and improve calibration:
- Ragi
- Moong
- Urad
- Sunflower
- Sesame
- Nigerseed
- Barley
- Masur
- Minor or highly localized crops with weak representation in the current pilot dataset

This shortened crop list keeps the first rule set aligned with the crops that are most important for Maharashtra agriculture and most likely to have reliable pattern signatures in the current data.
